## Hi there! 👋 I'm Anthony!

### About Me
I have always been a book-worm and loved understanding how things work. My passion for learning (and great food) somehow landed me a job as a baker. I worked as an artisan baker for over 3 years at an amazing sourdough bakery in Southern California (Shout out to [Village Bread](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Village+Bread+-+Bread+%26+Pastries/@34.0007716,-117.0595033,17.3z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x80db50be9b0bd825:0x8d3fcb374fb8e02c!8m2!3d34.0008083!4d-117.0595234!16s%2Fg%2F11cr_0v3y6?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDQwOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) - If you're ever in Calimesa, tell them Anthony says hello!)

When my fiancé (now wife! ❤️) landed a job across the country in New York City, it was a difficult decision to leave, but I knew it was a once in a lifetime experience.

Moving across the country, I knew this was the perfect opportunity to try something new. Having switched careers multiple times throughout my life, I couldn't pass up the opportunity to try something I've always been interested in. I have always wanted to work in Software, so I began my journey with online schooling as well as freelance work and applying what I've learned. 

When I'm not looking things up on Stack Overflow and Google, my interests are Video Games 🎮, Baking 🥖, Comics 💥 and Cats 🐈‍⬛. I have 4 adorable cats whom I have many pictures of and will share them if given the opportunity. 

To "sweeten" the deal for any potential employers out there, I bake some mean cookies and donuts. Just sayin. 🍪🍩 

If you want to know more, or just want to chat, my email is anthony.heredia.garcia@gmail.com. Look forward to hearing from you!
