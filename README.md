### About Me
I’m a Software Engineer with a passion for building solutions that are both functional and enjoyable to use. My career path has been unconventional—starting in finance, moving into artisan baking, and now thriving in software development.

Before transitioning into tech, I spent over three years as an artisan baker at [Village Bread](https://village-bread.restaurants-world.com/)—an amazing sourdough bakery in Southern California. (If you’re ever in Calimesa, tell them Anthony says hello!) That role honed my attention to detail, precision, and commitment to quality—skills that continue to shape my work as a developer.

After relocating to New York City, I seized the opportunity to pursue software engineering—combining online learning, freelance projects, and hands-on practice to build a strong foundation in full-stack development. Today, I work on projects that improve data workflows, system performance, and user experience.

Outside of coding, I enjoy video games 🎮, baking 🥖, comics 💥, and spending time with my four cats 🐈‍⬛. And yes, I still bake a mean sourdough.🍞
