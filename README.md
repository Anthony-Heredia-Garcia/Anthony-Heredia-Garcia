## Hi there! 👋 I'm Anthony Garcia

### About Me
I’m a Software Engineer who enjoys building systems that are **scalable, reliable, and pleasant to work with**—for both users and fellow developers. My career path hasn’t been linear, but it’s shaped how I approach engineering: thoughtfully, pragmatically, and with a strong respect for craft.

[Village Bread](https://village-bread.restaurants-world.com/) in Southern California. Working with sourdough taught me patience, precision, and how small process changes can dramatically impact outcomes—lessons I carry into system design, debugging, and performance work today.  
(If you’re ever in Calimesa, tell them Anthony says hello.)

After relocating to New York City, I transitioned into software engineering through a mix of online study, freelance work, and hands-on production experience. I now work full-time building and maintaining **cloud-based, API-driven systems** that support data ingestion, automation, and user-facing applications in the education space.

I’m especially interested in:

Outside of work, I enjoy video games 🎮, baking 🥖, comics 💥, and hanging out with my four cats 🐈‍⬛. I still bake a mean sourdough. 🍞
